# The pilot hour (docs/adr/0021): what `just pilot` records, in order. About 70 minutes of recording.
# Its job is to measure the real robot (`armpred sysid`: servo speed limit, lag, pipeline delay,
# reading ages) and to score relay-replay-servo against relay-replay on real recordings.
#
# One session per line, fields separated by |:
#   label | slider rad/s | minutes | motion | record-start options | what a person does first
# motion is teleop (someone in the headset) or drive:waypoints / drive:steps (just record-drive: the
# arm moves itself; close the headset app, since two senders on the joint port fight).
# Options are key=value, as `just record-start` takes them; quote a value with spaces or a ; in it.

teleop-2p5   | 2.5 | 8 | teleop | | Headset on, slider at 2.5. Teleoperate as usual: reach, place, return.
teleop-1p5   | 1.5 | 6 | teleop | | Slider at 1.5. Teleoperate as usual.
teleop-4     | 4.0 | 6 | teleop | | Slider at 4.0. Teleoperate as usual.
teleop-fast  | 6.0 | 5 | teleop | | Slider at its top (6.0). Large, quick moves across the whole reach: this is what shows the servo's speed limit.
payload-100g | 2.5 | 5 | teleop | load=payload payload_g=100 | Close the gripper on a ~100 g object (weigh it; if it is not 100 g, edit payload_g here first). Slider at 2.5.
hand-1       | 2.5 | 4 | teleop | load=hand | Slider at 2.5. A second person gently resists the arm by hand now and then: at most 2 s at a time, contact at most a fifth of the session.
live-150     | 2.5 | 5 | teleop | network=150ms-20j-0.5loss | Slider at 2.5. The robot now delays its own commands by ~150 ms with jitter and loss: teleoperate as usual.
live-sched   | 2.5 | 4 | teleop | "network=schedule:lan@0;150ms-20j-0.5loss@45;lan@90;50ms-5j@135" | Slider at 2.5. The delay changes by itself during the session: teleoperate as usual.
drive-steps-2p5 | 2.5 | 4 | drive:steps     | | Close the headset app (or leave it with motion off). Stand clear of the arm: it moves by itself now.
drive-steps-6   | 6.0 | 5 | drive:steps     | | Stay clear of the arm: these steps are fast.
drive-wp-2p5    | 2.5 | 5 | drive:waypoints | | Stay clear of the arm.
drive-wp-5      | 5.0 | 5 | drive:waypoints | | Stay clear of the arm.
drive-wp-sched  | 2.5 | 4 | drive:waypoints | "network=schedule:lan@0;150ms-20j-0.5loss@45;lan@90;50ms-5j@135" | Stay clear of the arm.
